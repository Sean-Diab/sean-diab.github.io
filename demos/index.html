Clash Royale AI

Solo project · reinforcement learning and computer vision

Agents that play full games of Clash Royale on their own, trained with reinforcement learning by playing against each other in a headless simulator of the game's battles, and a vision model that reads the board straight from video.

The top agent holds its tower in overtime

Watch the Hog Rider agent (top, red) defend its tower through an intense overtime finish against the X-Bow agent (bottom, blue). Every card is chosen and placed by the models; nobody touches the game.

With 20 seconds left, the X-Bow agent sets up its X-Bow at 4:40 and again at 4:52, and defends with Archers, Skeletons and The Log. The Hog agent holds with Cannon and Ice Golem and strikes back with one more Hog Rider. No tower falls before time runs out, so the tiebreaker drains the towers and the Hog agent wins 1–0 on tower health.

35 s, with sound · X-Bow model x1c, version 2600 · Hog model p2, version 4750.

Reading the board: units in a swarm

A detector looks at one frame of the arena at a time and finds every unit on it: where it stands, which side owns it, and what it is. Each box is a unit it found, colored by the side it gives (bottom, top); the label is its prediction, shown when it is right, so a box without a label is a unit it found but misnamed. This battle was held out of training; up to 31 units are on screen at once.

Reviser

Solo research project · 100M and 300M parameters

A small decoder-only language model that produces text as a sequence of cursor actions instead of only appending tokens: at each step it inserts a token at the cursor, moves the cursor left or right by some number of tokens, backspaces the token before it, or stops. Each action costs the same as one step of an ordinary language model, so an edit takes a few actions instead of retyping the whole text, and it can leave a gap while writing and come back to fill it.

FaceOff

Founder, solo-built · May–Sep 2026 · live on the App Store and Google Play

A real-time social and streaming app for Clash Royale players: broadcast your screen live with chat, talk to your opponent over voice during a match, and keep replay-backed battle history. Native SwiftUI (iOS) and Jetpack Compose (Android) apps on one FastAPI/PostgreSQL backend, with LiveKit/WebRTC for streaming and voice.

  • Recognizes the matched opponent from the screen capture (template matching and OCR) and connects both players to a voice room automatically.
  • Hardware H.264 screen sharing up to 1080p at 60 fps, recording without re-encoding, and replay clips aligned to battle timestamps.
  • Sign in with Apple and Google, push notifications, moderation, privacy controls, and nine languages including Arabic right-to-left.
